# Interior gallery: sources and image credits

Verified 23 October 2026. Runtime data is in `public/assets/sultra-gallery-data.js`. Every image is served locally from `public/images/culture/gallery/`; visiting a house does not send image requests to third-party hosts.

## Collection mapping

| House | Cultural context | Three frames |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Laika | Tolaki, Konawe context | Tenun Tolaki on a 2011 stamp; sinonggi; Lulo / Molulo |
| Mekongga | Tolaki, Mekongga / Kolaka context | The same three shared Tolaki traditions, explicitly labelled as shared |
| Bharugano Wuna | Muna | Historical clothing in the museum archive; kasuami shared regional food; rock art at Liang Metanduno |
| Banua Tada | Buton / Wolio | Clothing in a documented Posuo event; kasuami; a Buton weaver |

The descriptions are new summaries, with source links in each entry. Shared foods and Tolaki traditions are not presented as exclusively originating in a single house or regency. The gallery does not guess names or symbolic meanings for unidentified motifs or weapons.

Muna's archive is clearly labelled historical, with unknown date and photographer. The rock-art photograph adds regional cultural heritage, and is not described as a contemporary Muna adat motif. No age is claimed for the boat drawing: recent dating of a hand stencil at the same site must not be applied to every drawing there. Muna's historical photograph does show clothing accessories, but these are not labelled as a specific weapon type without an identification source.

Two available local options were intentionally excluded: an extremely small Tolaki wedding-clothing photograph (160 × 229 px), and a recently published government picture dictionary whose site explicitly says all rights reserved. Several Muna textile papers were found, but usable image files or permissions were insufficient for inclusion. The current selection uses actual photographs/documentation with explicit Commons license declarations; it contains no generated cultural photographs.

## Content references

- Tolaki clothing: [Dewi Suci Astuti, Universitas Halu Oleo, 2022](https://ojs.uho.ac.id/index.php/JPSB/article/view/28863). This documents several clothing categories rather than one universal costume.
- Tolaki context and the Konawe / Mekongga association: [Ministry of Tourism: Tolaki traditional clothes](https://www.indonesia.travel/id/id/travel-ideas/culture/tolaki-traditional-clothes).
- Sinonggi: [Ministry of Tourism](https://www.indonesia.travel/sa/en/travel-ideas/gastronomy/sinonggi) and [Universitas Terbuka report on traditional foods in Kendari](https://repository.ut.ac.id/6036/1/2012_191.pdf). Health claims on promotional pages are not reproduced.
- Lulo in Kolaka Utara: [Ahmad Muhlis, IAIN Parepare, 2020](https://repository.iainpare.ac.id/id/eprint/2011/). This is research using interviews, documents and observations.
- Lulo performance context: [Government of Kepulauan Selayar](https://kepulauanselayarkab.go.id/tari-lulo-sultra-tampil-pada-karnaval-budaya-hari-jadi-ke413-selayar-).
- Kasuami's regional spread: [Kementerian Pendidikan dan Kebudayaan, culinary reading, pp. 11–12](https://budikemdikbud.id/storage/book/5IBxkEvyCAIfTnrYwMgqS8GvY4gcpnAmfsNTYuQN.pdf). It explicitly mentions Buton, Muna and Wakatobi.
- Kasuami preparation: [BPTP Sulawesi Tenggara 2012 annual report, p. 71](https://ppid.pertanian.go.id/doc/238/Laporan%20Tahunan%20BPTP%20Sultra%202012.pdf).
- Muna textiles: [Syamsumarlin, Universitas Halu Oleo, 2018](https://sciencescholar.us/journal/index.php/ijssh/article/view/80). Text is summarized; no image from this paper is included.
- Muna rock art: [Marlon Ririmasse, Balai Arkeologi Ambon, 2007, p. 2](https://ejournal.brin.go.id/kapata/article/download/11788/9179/33225). Its discussion includes human figures, animals and boats in Muna.
- Wolio clothing and Posuo context: [Nur Alam Saleh, Balai Pelestarian Nilai Budaya](https://kebudayaan.kemdikbud.go.id/bpnbsulsel/perilaku-tata-krama-dalam-dan-luar-lingkungan-kerabat-pada-masyarakat-suku-bangsa-woliodi-kota-bau-bau-nur-alam-saleh/).

## Reuse requirements

Display each entry's `credit.author`, `credit.license`, linked `credit.url`, `credit.licenseUrl`, and `credit.changes` in the expanded information. Resized WebP derivatives retain the original applicable license. The file-page links below preserve descriptions and provenance; original download links are provided for maintenance.

Images keep their original aspect ratios. Files above 1280 px were resized to a maximum edge of 1280 px and encoded as WebP at quality 88. The 620 × 700 archive uses quality 92. Smaller sources are retained at their original resolution, not upscaled. This preserves authentic detail; it does not invent missing detail. The Molulo photograph intentionally has long-exposure motion blur in its original (1.6-second exposure), which is not a loading or texture-resolution bug.
